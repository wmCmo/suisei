Privacy

What Suisei stores, who can read it, and how to have it deleted. Last updated 29 October 2026.

Suisei is a form builder. Two kinds of people use it: someone who builds a form, and someone who answers one. What is stored differs, so they are described separately.

If you answer a form

You do not need an account and are not asked to identify yourself. What is stored is:

  • the answers you type or choose;
  • any hidden-field values carried in the link you followed — these are set by whoever shared the link, and are shown to you on request;
  • the time you submitted.

Your answers are readable only by the person who owns that form. This is enforced by the database itself, not by the page: a request carrying anything other than that person's credentials is refused.

No IP address, device identifier or location is recorded by Suisei, and nothing you type is kept before you press submit. Suisei's hosting providers keep their own server logs, which is outside its control.

If you build forms

Signing in uses your Google account. Suisei asks Google only for your email address and basic profile, and uses them to know which forms are yours. It cannot read your Google data, your mail, or anything else in your account.

Your forms — their questions, logic and settings — are stored alongside your account identifier. Other people signed in to Suisei cannot see them unless you publish a form, and even then they see the form, never its answers.

What Suisei does not do

  • No analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising, no profiling.
  • No cookies are set. A sign-in is remembered in your browser's own storage and is readable only by this site.
  • Nothing is sold, rented or shared with anyone for marketing.

Who else is involved

  • Supabase hosts the database and handles sign-in. Forms and answers are stored there.
  • Google is involved only when someone signs in to build.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr serve the typefaces and the icons. Loading a page requests files from them, which means they receive your IP address, as any website's asset host does.

Keeping and deleting

A form and its answers are kept until the person who owns the form deletes them. Deleting a form deletes its answers with it. Someone who owns forms can delete their account's data by deleting their forms.

If you answered someone's form and want that answer removed, ask the person who sent you the form — they control it. If you cannot reach them, write to the address below and it will be removed.
