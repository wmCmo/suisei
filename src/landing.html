Write a form the way you write a document.

Type a question. Press / for the next block. Branch it where it matters, publish it, and read what comes back — without once dragging a field out of a palette.

Open Suisei Read the source

Free and open source. Your forms and the answers to them live in your own database, not in someone's product.

Three things it does differently.

One

A block menu, not a form builder

Every question is a line you type. Press / and the block menu opens where the caret is — eighteen kinds, filtered as you type, chosen with Enter. The form reads like the document you were already writing.

Two

Logic that sits in the page

A rule lives under the question it belongs to, not on a separate canvas you have to keep in sync. Jump to a page, show or hide a block, or end the form early — and a hidden field from the link can decide it too.

Three

Two runners, one form

The same block list renders as a page of questions or as one question at a time, and whoever is answering can switch between them mid-form without losing a single answer.
